From the field

AI in sales work, measured where it matters.

AI-assisted sales workflows are the speciality within Talja's AI advisory. Here is the result of eight months of live sales work.

See what the workflow would do for you

From early October 2025 through late May 2026, Talja's founder built and ran an AI-assisted sales workflow inside an international B2B sales organisation serving enterprise clients. AI handled account research, call prioritisation and the preparation for every call. Every call was made by a human.

Process

How it works.

No discovery theater and no 40-page reports. Three steps from first message to finished work.

01 / 03

  1. 01 Pin down the need

    Describe your situation by email or through the form. Together we pin down the goal, the current state and the key constraints.

  2. 02 Choose a direction

    You get a proposal covering scope, priorities and way of working. Based on that, you decide how we proceed.

  3. 03 Carry it out

    We proceed as agreed with advisory, execution or a combination. Choices are made visible, and you always know the next step.
