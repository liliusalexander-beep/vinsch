FAQ

Questions every sensible business owner asks

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly — you'll get a straight answer within one business day.

We're not technical at all. Is that a problem?

It's the normal case — most of our clients don't have anyone technical on staff. We handle the technology end to end and explain everything in plain language. You never need to learn jargon to work with us.

What does a typical project cost?

As a guide: website projects typically start around 30,000 SEK, internal AI tools and automations from 25,000 SEK, and ongoing support plans from 2,500 SEK per month. Every project gets a written fixed quote before we start — the consultation that produces it is free.

How long until we see results?

Most websites and first tools launch within two to six weeks. We deliberately start with the workflow where time savings are most obvious, so you can measure the difference within the first month of using it.

Is our data safe with AI tools?

Yes — and we treat this as non-negotiable. Your data is stored in the EU, processed under GDPR, and never used to train public AI models. We'll walk you through exactly where every piece of information lives, in writing, before anything goes live.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?

We design for it. Anything customer-facing or financial gets a human checkpoint: the AI drafts, your team approves. We test against your real cases before launch and tune the tools during the first weeks of use.

Do you only work with companies in Stockholm?

We're based in Stockholm and happy to meet in person here, but we work with businesses across Sweden and the Nordics. Websites, tools, and support all work just as well over video and remote access.
