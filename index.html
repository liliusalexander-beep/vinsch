Process

How a project runs.

No discovery theater, no 40-page decks. A clear rope from first call to working software.

01 / 04

  1. 01 Listen

    A free 30-minute call, then a short visit if it helps. We map where hours leak out of your week and what they cost you. You talk, we take notes.

  2. 02 Plan

    You get a fixed quote in plain language: what we will build, what it costs, what it saves and when it lands. No meter running, no surprises on the invoice.

  3. 03 Build

    Working software in weeks, not quarters. You see progress every Friday and can change course while it is still cheap to do so.

  4. 04 Lift

    We train your team in an afternoon, stay for the wobbly first weeks and keep improving the setup as your business grows. Support is the product, not an afterthought.
